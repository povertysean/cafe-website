Welcome to our cafe, a place where coffee, community, creativity, and comfort come together in North Jakarta.

Our Vision: To be a beloved community hub where passion for coffee and connections flourish.

Our Full Story & Mission

Exquisite Beans for Your Daily Grind

Founded in North Jakarta, The Daily Grind Cafe started with a simple belief: that coffee has the power to bring people together. Every cup we serve is a testament to our dedication to quality, sustainability, and craftsmanship.

Our Mission:
• Source and roast the finest beans with care.
• Craft exceptional beverages that delight the senses.
• Champion sustainability in all our practices.

Together, we brew. Together, we connect.
